Delete Your Data

KinetiPix has no accounts and never sends data off your phone, so all of your data is on your device and you can delete it yourself at any time. We hold no copy.

Delete a single photo

  1. Open KinetiPix.
  2. In the carousel at the bottom, long-press the photo.
  3. Tap Delete and confirm.

The photo and everything linked to it are removed from the device.

Delete everything

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → KinetiPix.
  2. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage.

Or simply uninstall KinetiPix. Both remove all photos, animations and settings the app stored.
